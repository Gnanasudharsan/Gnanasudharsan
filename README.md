## 01 / About Me

I'm **Gnanasudharsan Ashokumar**, a Boston-based engineer working at the intersection of **AI/ML systems, data engineering, data science, and applied research**.

- Master's student in **Data Analytics Engineering at Northeastern University** (GPA: **3.94/4.0**; expected graduation: **December 2026**).
- Previously a **Data & AI Engineer Co-op at Intelligent DataWorks**, working on retrieval-augmented generation, LLM workflows, APIs, authentication, and PostgreSQL-backed applications.
- Previously a **Machine Learning Engineer at Flare Innovations**, developing computer vision and IoT-enabled automation systems.
- Interested in **scalable data platforms, applied machine learning, MLOps, LLM applications, model evaluation, and dependable production systems**.
- Co-author of **10 IEEE research publications** spanning AI, deep learning, computer vision, IoT, blockchain, and applied engineering.

I focus on solutions that are **reproducible, measurable, maintainable, and useful in practice**. Explore my repositories for implementations, experiments, and documentation.
